How the numbers were taken

Plain numbers are mine, from an M3 Pro in October 2026: resident memory summed across every process the app runs, at idle, and du -sh of the app bundle. RSS over-counts shared memory, so treat them as rough. Anything with ~ is a typical figure for an app I don't have installed.

What counts as a swap

The replacement has to cover the daily job without an account, a subscription, or a background daemon it didn't need before. Sometimes the win is weight. Sometimes it's that the files are yours. Each row says which.

Source

The list lives in src/_data/lightweightApps.yaml. Rows get added when I've used the replacement long enough to know what it lost.
