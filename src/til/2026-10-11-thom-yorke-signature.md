Thom Yorke doesn't have a conventional autograph. His signature is literally `thom y.` with the "o" turned into a bug-eyed, sharp-toothed monster doodle straight out of a *Kid A* sketchbook.

The best part is seeing it in the wild: on his Wikipedia infobox, sitting right below the formal bio under a completely deadpan header that just says **Signature**.
